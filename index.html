How It Works

A Different Kind of Physical Therapy

Most PT plans hand you a folder of exercises. Roman's approach puts the work into his hands. Healing happens during the session, not after it.

Hands-On, From Start to Finish

Every session is skilled manual therapy, targeted massage, and joint work performed directly by Roman. No machines, no generic printouts, just focused hands-on treatment for exactly what's bothering you.

Active Massage, Not Assignments

The therapeutic work happens on the table through active, hands-on massage technique. You don't need to keep up a routine at home to see results.

Shaped Around Your Body

Every visit starts with how you're actually feeling that day, so treatment adjusts session to session instead of following a fixed script.

About Roman

Over 5 Years of Hands-On Practice

Roman has spent the last 5 years using skilled manual therapy to help people move and feel better, without turning recovery into a second job. His background combines licensed physical therapy training with advanced massage and manual technique.

Patients come to him after years of chronic tension, post-injury stiffness, or PT programs that can be overbearing.
