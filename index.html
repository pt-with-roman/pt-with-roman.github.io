How It Works

A Different Kind of Physical Therapy

Most PT plans hand you a folder of exercises. Roman's approach puts the work into his hands, not your evenings — so healing happens during the session, not after it.

Hands-On, From Start to Finish

Every session is skilled manual therapy — active, targeted massage and joint work performed directly by Roman. No machines, no generic printouts, just focused hands-on treatment for exactly what's bothering you.

Active Massage, Not Assignments

The therapeutic work happens on the table through active, hands-on massage technique — you don't need to keep up a routine at home to see results.

Shaped Around Your Body

Every visit starts with how you're actually feeling that day, so treatment adjusts session to session instead of following a fixed script.

About Roman

15 Years of Hands-On Practice

Roman has spent the last 15 years focused on one thing: using skilled manual therapy to help people move and feel better, without turning recovery into a second job. His background combines licensed physical therapy training with advanced massage and manual technique — so every session blends clinical assessment with genuinely therapeutic, active bodywork.

Patients come to him after years of chronic tension, post-injury stiffness, or PT programs that felt more like homework than healing. His approach is simple: figure out what's actually restricted, then work it loose, by hand, in the room.
